# How Much of This Is Me?

**A language-model card game for two players**

In this game, you decide whether to take sentences written by a language model as your own. The player who accepts more sentences wins. But every sentence you accept will be published under your name.

## Setup

- Each player has two spaces for cards: **Diary** and **Candidates**.
- Prepare cards, something to write with, and a device that can access a language model.
- Before playing, agree on how the completed diaries will be made public.

## How to Play

### 1. Write down what happened

Each player lists events from the past three days. Write one item per card and place the cards in your **Diary**.

### 2. Have a language model add to your diary

Give the language model all the sentences in your **Diary** and ask it to “Add to this diary.” Have it generate 10 sentences.

### 3. Choose sentences your opponent may hesitate to accept

From the 10 sentences generated for your opponent, choose 5 that you think they might hesitate to add to their own **Diary**. Place those cards in your opponent's **Candidates**.

### 4. Decide what to take as your own

Each player reads the 5 sentences in their **Candidates**, one at a time, and decides whether to claim each sentence as their own and add it to their **Diary**.

- **TAKE: 1 point.** Move the sentence to your **Diary**.
- **PASS: 0 points.** Do not add the sentence to your **Diary**.

After both players have decided on all 5 sentences, compare scores. The player with more points wins. A tie is a draw.

### 5. Publish your diary

Each player publishes every sentence in their Diary under their own name.

---

The more you try to win, the more sentences generated by a language model you must take as your own. Earning a point and leaving words you did not write under your own name become part of the same decision.
