# How Much of This Is Me?

**＜言語モデル・カードゲーム〉どこまでが、わたし？**  
A two-player language-model card game by Toru Urakawa (2026).

Players write short diary entries about the past three days. A language model generates ten additional sentences for each diary, and the other player chooses five that may be difficult to accept as one's own. Players score a point for each sentence they take into their diary. At the end, each player publishes their diary under their own name.

The more points players try to earn, the more words they did not write they must claim as their own.
